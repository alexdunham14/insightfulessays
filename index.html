Anton Reicha: The String Quartets #

Ron Drummond, classical.net, archived copy

Anton Reicha was an exact contemporary of Beethoven and his own highly individual and ambitious conception of what instrumental music could be after Haydn, but that music was largely forgotten, and most accounts of Reicha fail to depict what's interesting about it. Drummond turned me on to Reicha almost a decade ago, and now I am obsessed.
